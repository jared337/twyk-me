3D Print a Personalised Christmas Bauble With Names (70 mm)

A personalised Christmas tree decoration: a flat bauble 70 mm across with the children's names, Mia and Leo, in raised script, little stars, and a loop to hang it. Flat, so it prints fast with no supports. Change the names to yours.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes does this project use?

70 mm across, 4 mm thick with rounded edges, letters 13 mm tall standing 1.5 mm up, a 10 mm hanging loop.

What print settings should I use?

PLA (silk or glitter filament looks great), 0.2 mm layers, swap colour at 4 mm for the names.

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
