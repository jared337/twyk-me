How it works

A full 3D editor, in the tab you already have open

Every model on twyk.me is built with these tools, and every published model opens back up in them. Drag shapes, punch holes, size things in millimetres, export to your printer.

Read the step-by-step guide →

A shape for everything

Boxes, rounded solids, cylinders, cones, tapers, wedges and tubes, plus a searchable library. Click to drop one on the workplane and start.

Solid or hole

Flip any shape into a hole and merge to cut it away. Complex cavities, threads and slots come from the same simple shapes.

Millimetre precision

Exact size, position and rotation in mm, an adjustable snap grid, rulers and keep-aspect resize. Parts fit because you told them to.

Move, rotate, resize, push

Direct handles for every transform, plus push face to pull a single face in or out without rebuilding the shape.

Group, merge, split, cut

Combine shapes into reusable groups, merge them into one solid, or split and cut to take a design apart again.

Align & duplicate

One-click alignment on any axis and smart duplicate for rows, stacks and radial patterns. No more nudging by eye.

Colour per part

Give every shape its own colour, ready for multi-colour printing and clean, readable assemblies.

Import & export

Bring in existing STL files, export clean STL or 3MF that slices the first time. Your work is never locked in.
