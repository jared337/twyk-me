Made to fit

Type a size. Get exactly that size.

Fit can’t be a guess. Every shape in twyk has real dimensions you can type, so a part made for a 72 mm phone is 72.00 mm, not roughly 72.

  • Millimetres or inches, snapping down to 0.1 mm.
  • A ruler that measures the gaps between parts, and undo that survives a reload.
  • New to 3D? See how it works in four steps.
