3D Print a Replacement Blind Clip (15 mm, 4 mm Pin Hole)

Replacing a small broken plastic part: a clip for a blind, 15 mm wide, with a C-shaped hook that snaps onto a 10 mm rail and a 4 mm hole for its pin. The same steps work for most small clips and brackets: measure the old one with calipers, then copy its sizes.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes does this project use?

15 mm wide, 30 mm long, a hook for a 10 mm rail with 2.5 mm walls, a 4 mm pin hole.

What print settings should I use?

PETG (it flexes instead of snapping), 0.16 mm layers, 100% infill for a small part, printed on its side.

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
