3D Print a Personalised Desk Plaque Gift ("World's Best Dad")

A personalised desk plaque, a gift you make yourself: "WORLD'S BEST DAD" and his name in raised letters inside a border, on a plaque that prints flat, and a separate stand with an angled slot to hold it up.

At a glance

Sizes
plaque 150 x 75 x 5 mm, letters 1.5 mm high, stand 120 mm wide with a 5.6 mm slot leaning back 15 degrees. Change the name to anyone's.
Print settings
PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports. Swap filament at the letters for a second colour.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes does this project use?

plaque 150 x 75 x 5 mm, letters 1.5 mm high, stand 120 mm wide with a 5.6 mm slot leaning back 15 degrees. Change the name to anyone's.

What print settings should I use?

PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports. Swap filament at the letters for a second colour.

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
