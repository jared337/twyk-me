twyk vs Tinkercad

Tinkercad and twyk are both free, browser-based 3D modellers built on the same idea: drop solid shapes on a workplane and cut them with holes. If you know Tinkercad, you can use twyk on day one. The difference is what twyk adds on top: tools like Sculpt, Formula, Bend, a flex test and a clearance check take you well beyond boxes. Tinkercad is still the better pick for classrooms and for electronics.

✓ Choose twyk if…

  • You want organic shapes, springs or lattices that are hard to make from boxes and cylinders.
  • You print parts that have to work, and want to test a flexible part or check moving parts before printing.
  • You want 3MF export with separate, named, coloured parts for multi-colour printing.

Choose Tinkercad if…

  • You’re teaching a class and need mature classroom management and lesson content.
  • You want electronics simulation (Circuits) or block-based coding (Codeblocks) alongside 3D design.

Moving from Tinkercad to twyk

Export your Tinkercad design as an STL (or SVG for flat shapes) and import it into twyk. It arrives as one solid you can scale, cut and add to. Use Split to take it apart into shapes where possible, or add your own shapes to it. See importing and exporting, or how to bring in models from any site.

What twyk adds

  • 80+ shapes and 48 fonts, each with settings you can change later. See the shapes
  • Exact sizes in millimetres or inches, a ruler for gaps, and X-ray for hidden parts. See the precision tools
  • Sculpt, Formula and Bend make organic, mathematical and curved shapes. See the shaping tools
  • Flex and Gaps test flexible parts and print-in-place clearances before you print. See the print checks

Frequently asked questions

Is twyk a good Tinkercad alternative?

Yes. twyk uses the same solid-and-hole approach, so Tinkercad users feel at home, and adds Sculpt, Formula, Bend, X-ray, a flex test and a clearance check. Both are free.

Can I move my Tinkercad designs to twyk?

Yes. Export them from Tinkercad as STL or SVG and import them into twyk, up to 64 MB. Split can take an imported model apart into editable shapes.

Is twyk free like Tinkercad?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account.

Which is better for teaching?

Tinkercad, today. Its Classes feature, student accounts and lesson content are built for classrooms, and twyk doesn't have classroom tools yet.
