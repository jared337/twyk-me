Smooth or Blocky? Sculpting a Penguin in twyk vs Tinkercad

A chubby penguin figure starts as two spheres: a body and a head. We made it in Tinkercad and in twyk, filmed for real, with a clock on screen. Tinkercad stacks and groups the spheres, but has no tool to melt shapes together, so the neck is a hard crease. twyk's Sculpt blends them into one smooth piece, like clay, in one step.
