From blank workplane to printed part

twyk is a full 3D editor that runs in your browser. Here is the whole workflow, start to finish, using the actual product.

1 Choose your shapes

Every design starts in the shape library

Click a shape to drop it on the workplane. The library goes far past the basics, organised into ten categories:

  • Basic & Curved for boxes, cylinders, cones and smooth solids
  • Polyhedra & Profiles for dice, prisms and extrusion outlines
  • Symbols, Parts, Home, Building and Fun for ready-made pieces
  • Text for names, labels and embossing

There is also a search box when you know what you're after.

2 Dial in the details

Real dimensions, not guesswork

Select any shape and the properties panel gives you exact control:

  • Solid or Hole — flip a shape into a cutter to carve cavities and slots
  • Size in mm on all three axes, with keep-aspect and resize %
  • Position and rotation typed in exactly, or dragged with handles
  • Colour per shape, ready for multi-colour printing

Parts fit the first time because you told them exactly how big to be.

3 Combine and edit

One toolbar for the whole build

Move, rotate and resize with direct handles, then shape the design with the tools that do the heavy lifting:

  • Group shapes to move them as one, Merge to fuse them into a single solid
  • Align on any axis and Duplicate for repeats
  • Split and Cut to take a design apart, Push face to pull a single face in or out

And the "Saved" badge on the left is doing exactly what it says: your work saves as you go.

4 Export or publish

Straight to your slicer, or straight to the world

When the design is done, export it in the format your workflow needs:

  • Export 3MF — keeps the separate objects, ideal for multi-colour printing
  • Export STL — one mesh that opens in any slicer or CAD tool

Or publish it to twyk.me instead: your model gets a live 3D page, a gallery, print files and a place on your creator profile — and anyone can open it back up in the editor and remix it, with credit linking back to you.
