1 Start

Start from nothing, a file, or someone else’s model

Sign in with Google and you land on Home: your models, and what’s trending in the Community. Everything saves to your account about a second after you change it.

  • New model for an empty workplane, or Duplicate to try an idea on a copy.
  • Import an STL, 3MF (with its colours) or SVG up to 64 MB, like a model you downloaded from Thingiverse, Printables or MakerWorld.
  • Make my own copy of any model shared in the Community, and change it however you like.

2 Build

Drop in shapes. Cut with holes. Merge.

Click a shape and it lands on the workplane. The library goes far past boxes and cylinders: ten categories, from gears and stars to symbols, parts, home and building pieces, plus text.

  • Search understands everyday words, so “donut” finds the torus.
  • Every shape stays editable: a gear can get more teeth, a star more points, long after you placed it.
  • Solid, Hole or Hollow: turn a shape into a hole, then Merge to cut it into the part.
  • Group parts to move them together, or Merge them into one solid.

3 Make it exact

Type the size. Get exactly that size.

Parts that fit first time aren’t luck. Drag the grips for a rough size, then type the real numbers.

  • Grips on every object: corners resize width and depth, the top grip sets the height, the arcs turn it.
  • Click a measurement on the model and type a new value, or use the properties panel.
  • Millimetres or inches, snapping from 0.1 to 10 mm, with plate presets for common printers.
  • The ruler shows the size, the distance to the nearest parts, or the margins inside what it sits on.
  • Align in nine ways on all three axes, mirror in place, or space parts evenly.

4 Shape it

Go far beyond blocks

The Tools tab is where models get interesting. Every tool shows a see-through preview that updates as you change its settings, and nothing changes until you press Apply. Esc leaves the model exactly as it was.

Melt two or more shapes into one smooth piece, like clay, with joins that flow instead of seams. Set how soft or sharp the blend is.

5 Check it prints

Know it will print before you waste filament

Built for the problems that actually ruin prints: parts that snap, parts that fuse, and parts you can’t see to fix.

Flex

Bend it in the computer first. It says whether it springs back, stays bent or snaps, in PLA, PETG or TPU, and where.

Gaps

Lists every pair of parts that touch or come too close, so hinges and print-in-place pieces come off the bed apart.

X-ray

Everything else turns see-through, so a part hidden inside another can be picked, moved and cut.

Hidden pockets

Seal a magnet, a nut or a weight inside a print, exactly where you measured it.
6 Export

Straight to your slicer, in colour

When it’s ready, the last few clicks:

  1. Merge any holes into their solids.
  2. Run Gaps on anything that moves, and Flex on anything that bends.
  3. Lay flat: click a face and the part turns so that side is down.
  4. Export 3MF to keep separate, named, coloured parts, or STL for one mesh that opens anywhere.
  5. Open it in your slicer. It’s already in millimetres.

7 Share

Share it, and let others make it theirs

Your models stay private until you choose to share them. Share one to the Community and anyone can open it, like it, and make their own copy to change for their gear.

  • Trending and recent models, ready to copy.
  • Make my own copy opens a shared model as your own, every shape still editable.
  • Kept safe: anyone can report a problem, and moderators check what’s reported.
