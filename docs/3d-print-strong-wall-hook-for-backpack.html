3D Print a Strong Wall Hook for a Backpack (Holds 5 kg)

A wall hook strong enough for a loaded backpack: a thick arm with an upturned tip, two counterbored screw holes, and the most important part for strength: which way round it is printed.

Start building → Watch on YouTube

At a glance

Sizes
back plate 40 x 100 x 8 mm, an arm 25 mm wide, 14 mm thick and 60 mm long, holes for 4 mm screws. Use wall plugs that suit your wall.
Print settings
PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 5 walls, 40% infill, printed lying on its side (layers along the hook).

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
