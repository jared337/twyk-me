Free 3D modelling for 3D printing, right in your browser

twyk is a free 3D modelling tool that runs in your browser and is made for 3D printing. There is nothing to install: sign in with Google and start building. This five-minute tour builds real, printable parts and shows the whole toolbox, from dropping shapes and cutting holes to testing a flexible ring and exporting a 3MF with plates for your slicer.

What you will make

  • A mounting plate with bolt holes
  • A flexi ring, flex-tested in PLA
  • A vent cover from honeycomb tiles
  • A lighthouse built with code

Step by step

  1. Shapes, holes and Merge

    Drop shapes onto the plate, cut holes through them and Merge the result into one solid. The tour builds a mounting plate with bolt holes in under a minute.

  2. Text and fonts

    Add text in 48 fonts, with Hebrew and English together the right way round, so a name tag or sign takes seconds.

  3. Formula shapes

    Springs, knots, seashells and gyroid lattices come straight from maths, with settings you can change later.

  4. Tiles

    Infill-pattern panels such as honeycomb, turned here into a vent cover.

  5. Repeat and Flex test

    Repeat a shape in a ring to make a flexi ring, then run the flex test to see whether it bends or snaps in PLA before you spend filament.

  6. Hollow, Lip, Cut and X-ray

    The tools that exist because a model has to print: hollow a shape out, roll a rim, cut a model in two and see inside it with X-ray.

  7. Sculpt and Bend

    Melt shapes together like clay with Sculpt, or twist, bend and taper a shape with Bend.

  8. AI twyk

    Describe what you want and AI twyk builds it with the same tools you use, so you can edit or undo every step. In the video its building is sped up 8x and 12x.

  9. Build with code

    Detailed models, like a lighthouse, are written as one program and checked before they are handed over.

  10. Plates and export

    Arrange parts on plates and export a 3MF ready for Bambu Studio, or an STL for any slicer.

  11. Community

    Share a model, or make your own private copy of one someone else shared and change it.

Frequently asked questions

Is twyk really free?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Do I need to install anything?

No. twyk runs in your web browser, so there is nothing to download or install.

What can I export to my slicer?

twyk exports 3MF, with separate, named, coloured parts and plates, or STL for any slicer. Sizes are always in millimetres.

Where should a complete beginner start?

Start with the desk caddy tutorial, the first in the twyk video series. It builds a real model step by step and covers the editor properly.
