Design should end in your hands

twyk.me exists because the distance between "I have an idea" and "I'm holding it" is still too long. A full 3D editor in the browser, a community of models you can copy and change, and a printer at the end of it.

What we believe

Three things we won't compromise on

1

Your files are yours

Everything you design exports as clean STL or 3MF, any time, no strings. If twyk disappeared tomorrow, your work wouldn't. Lock-in is not a business model we want.

2

Remixing is how people learn

Nobody's first design is great, and nobody learns from a file they can't open. Every shared model can be copied into your own models and changed.

3

The print is the point

We measure success in objects, not downloads. Everything about twyk, from mm-exact sizing to slicer-ready exports, is built so the thing on your screen becomes a thing on your desk.

The story so far

First the editor, now the home for what it makes

twyk started as an answer to a simple frustration: browser 3D tools were either toys that couldn't make real parts, or CAD packages that took a semester to learn. So we built the editor we wanted — approachable in the first ten minutes, precise enough for parts that fit.

Every shape in twyk keeps its real dimensions, so you can keep refining a design to the millimetre. Shared to the Community, a design becomes something others can copy, change and print.

We're just getting started. If that sounds like your kind of thing, say hello.
