twyk vs 3D Slash

3D Slash and twyk are both easy ways into 3D design, but they build in very different ways. 3D Slash works like Minecraft: you add and carve away blocks, which feels natural to young makers. twyk works with smooth shapes and holes measured in millimetres, and AI twyk can build a model from a description or photo. twyk is free with no paid tier; 3D Slash has a free tier with queued downloads and paid plans for more.

✓ Choose twyk if…

  • You want smooth shapes and exact millimetre sizes for parts that fit.
  • You’d like AI to build the first version from a description or photo.
  • You want everything free, with no queues or paid tiers.
  • You print functional parts and want to test them before printing.

Choose 3D Slash if…

  • You’re introducing young makers to 3D, and block-by-block building feels natural to them.
  • You want an offline app.

Moving from 3D Slash to twyk

Export from 3D Slash as STL and import it into twyk. Then smooth things out by adding shapes, cut it, or ask AI twyk to rebuild it as clean, editable shapes. See importing and exporting, or how to bring in models from any site.

Frequently asked questions

Is twyk easier than 3D Slash?

3D Slash's block-building has the gentlest start, which is why it suits young children. twyk is also designed to be easy, and with AI twyk you can simply describe what you want.

Is twyk free?

Yes, completely, with no plans, payments or download queues. 3D Slash has a free tier, with paid plans from $2 a month for immediate downloads, more colours and the offline app.

Can 3D Slash make a model from a photo?

We couldn't find a built-in feature for that as of October 2026. In twyk, AI twyk can build from up to three pictures.

Can I bring a 3D Slash model into twyk?

Yes. Export it as STL and import it into twyk, up to 64 MB.
