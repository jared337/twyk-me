3D Print a Two-Tier Spice Rack for 6 Jars (Fits a 300 mm Cupboard)

A stepped spice rack for six jars 45 mm wide: two tiers of three, so the back row stands higher and you can read every label. It is 166 mm wide, which fits inside a 300 mm cupboard with room to spare.

Start building → Watch on YouTube

At a glance

Print settings
PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports.

Build details

Stand-in jars are used to check the fit before printing, then deleted. Sizes: shelves 158 x 52 mm, the top tier 40 mm higher, rails 12 mm tall to stop jars sliding off.

Frequently asked questions

What print settings should I use?

PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports.

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
