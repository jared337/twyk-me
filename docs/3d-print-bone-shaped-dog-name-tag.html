3D Print a Bone-Shaped Dog Name Tag (Name on the Front, Phone on the Back)

A bone-shaped name tag for a dog: the name raised on the front in its own colour, and your phone number engraved into the back, with a hole for the ring. Built from a box and four cylinders.

At a glance

Sizes
50 x 25 mm, 4 mm thick, a 5 mm hole for the split ring. The phone number in the video is a made-up example: use yours.
Print settings
PETG (tough, and fine in the rain), 0.12 mm layers for crisp letters, 100% infill.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes does this project use?

50 x 25 mm, 4 mm thick, a 5 mm hole for the split ring. The phone number in the video is a made-up example: use yours.

What print settings should I use?

PETG (tough, and fine in the rain), 0.12 mm layers for crisp letters, 100% infill.

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
