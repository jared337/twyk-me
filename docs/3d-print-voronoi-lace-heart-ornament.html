3D Print a Voronoi Lace Heart Ornament (70 mm, No Supports)

A heart-shaped ornament filled with organic Voronoi lace, with a solid edge round it and a loop to hang it by. Four tools working together: a Voronoi tile, a frame made into a hole to trim the tile to the heart, Merge, and Cutter & mold for the edge. Any shape works in place of the heart.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes does this project use?

70 mm across, 4 mm thick, lace cells 10 mm with 1.6 mm walls, a 2.5 mm solid edge, a 10 mm hanging loop.

What print settings should I use?

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
