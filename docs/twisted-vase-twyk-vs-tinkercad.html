A Twisted Vase in twyk vs Tinkercad: Twist, Taper, Hollow and a Rolled Rim

A twisted vase, made in Tinkercad and in twyk, filmed for real with a clock on screen. Tinkercad has no twist tool of its own, so we used a community shape generator, Twisted polygon, and hollowed it with a smaller copy set to Hole. It can't taper the vase or roll its rim. twyk does all four with its own tools: Bend twists and tapers, Hollow gives an even wall with the top open, and Lip rolls the rim.
