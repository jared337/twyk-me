3D Print a Cute Chubby Penguin Figure (70 mm, Sculpted by Hand)

A chubby penguin figure, 70 mm tall, sculpted by hand from spheres: the body and head melted together into one smooth shape with Sculpt, then a white belly, eyes, a beak, feet and wings, each in its own colour.

Frequently asked questions

What print settings should I use?

PLA, 0.12 mm layers for a smooth surface, 2 walls, 10% infill, supports only under the wings if your printer needs them.

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
