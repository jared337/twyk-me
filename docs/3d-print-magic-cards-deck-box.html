3D Print a Deck Box for 100 Sleeved Magic Cards (Custom Lid)

A deck box for 100 sleeved Magic: The Gathering cards (or any standard-size card game): a box with a thumb notch to get the cards out, and a lid that slides over it with DRAGONS engraved on top.

Project Gear & hobby ⏱ 2:28
Start building → Watch on YouTube

At a glance

Sizes
inside 70 x 68 x 95 mm, for cards in standard sleeves (66.5 x 91.5 mm) and a 100-card stack about 65 mm thick. The lid is 0.3 mm bigger all round, so it slides on and stays on.
Print settings
PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports. Print the lid top down.

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
