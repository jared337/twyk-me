3D Print a Dog-Shaped Cookie Cutter (80 mm)

A cookie cutter in the shape of a sitting dog, 80 mm wide. A dog's outline is hard to make from simple shapes, so this one is asked of AI twyk, twyk's modelling assistant (the waiting is sped up). Then the size is checked by hand, with the ruler.

At a glance

Print settings
PLA (food-safe filament if it touches dough often), 0.2 mm layers, no supports, printed rim down.

Build details

A cutter needs a thin cutting edge (0.8 mm here) and a wider rim on top to press on, and no base.

Frequently asked questions

What print settings should I use?

PLA (food-safe filament if it touches dough often), 0.2 mm layers, no supports, printed rim down.

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
