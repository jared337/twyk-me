3D Print a GoPro Handlebar Mount for a 32 mm Bar (Standard 3-Prong)

A GoPro mount that clamps onto a 32 mm bike handlebar: a split ring tightened with an M3 bolt, and the standard GoPro three-prong mount on top, for the usual 5 mm thumbscrew.

Project Gear & hobby ⏱ 2:57
Start building → Watch on YouTube

At a glance

Sizes
ring 33 mm inside (32 mm bar + 1 mm for a rubber strip), 4 mm walls, 20 mm wide. Prongs 3 mm thick with 3.2 mm gaps, 15 mm across the three, rounded to a 7.5 mm radius, with a 5.2 mm hole for the thumbscrew.
Print settings
PETG or ASA, 0.16 mm layers, 5 walls, 50% infill, printed on its side so the prongs are strong.
