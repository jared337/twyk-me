3D Print a Replacement Oven Knob (6 mm D-Shaft, 35 mm)

A replacement knob for an oven or cooker with the common 6 mm D-shaped shaft: 35 mm across, with grip ridges, a pointer line, and a D-shaped hole that only goes on one way round, so the pointer always reads true.

At a glance

Sizes
35 mm across, 22 mm tall, 18 grip ridges, a 6.2 mm hole with its flat 4.6 mm from the round side (the usual D-shaft). Measure your shaft first: some are 6.35 mm (1/4 inch).
Print settings
PETG or ASA (a knob near the oven gets warm), 0.16 mm layers, 4 walls, 40% infill, no supports.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes does this project use?

35 mm across, 22 mm tall, 18 grip ridges, a 6.2 mm hole with its flat 4.6 mm from the round side (the usual D-shaft). Measure your shaft first: some are 6.35 mm (1/4 inch).

What print settings should I use?

PETG or ASA (a knob near the oven gets warm), 0.16 mm layers, 4 walls, 40% infill, no supports.

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
