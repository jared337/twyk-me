3D Print a Car Air Vent Phone Holder (Fits 80 mm Phones in a Case)

A phone holder for the car that clips onto horizontal air vent slats: a cradle for a phone up to 80 mm wide and 14 mm thick with its case on, and two fingers with hooks that slide between the slats and catch behind them.

At a glance

Sizes
cradle 92 mm wide, side arms 84 mm apart, a 16 mm deep ledge, two 25 mm vent fingers 40 mm apart.
Print settings
PETG (it gets hot in a car), 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 25% infill, printed on its side so the fingers are strong.
