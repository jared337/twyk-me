3D Print a Planter for a 90 mm Pot, With Drip Tray and Name

A planter for a 90 mm nursery pot, with a rolled rim, drain holes, the plant's name standing out on the front, and a matching drip tray printed on a second plate.

Project Home & kitchen ⏱ 3:06
Start building → Watch on YouTube

At a glance

Sizes
100 mm across and 85 mm tall, 2.4 mm walls (95 mm inside, for a 90 mm pot), three 8 mm drain holes, a drip tray 116 mm across and 14 mm tall.
Print settings
PETG (it sits in water), 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill. The name prints as part of the wall.

What sizes does this project use?

What print settings should I use?

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
