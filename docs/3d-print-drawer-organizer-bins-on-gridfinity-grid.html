3D Print Drawer Organizer Bins on the Gridfinity Grid (3 x 2, Knife Slot)

A set of drawer bins sized to the 42 mm Gridfinity grid, filling a 3 x 2 space: a long bin for pens, three small bins, and a block with a slot that holds a Stanley utility knife upright.

Start building → Watch on YouTube

At a glance

Print settings
PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 2 walls, 15% infill, no supports.

Build details

The bins use the Gridfinity sizes (41.5 mm per unit, 3.75 mm corners, 7 mm height units) with flat bottoms, so they sit straight in a drawer. To lock into a Gridfinity baseplate, they would need its stepped feet added.

Frequently asked questions

What print settings should I use?

PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 2 walls, 15% infill, no supports.

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
