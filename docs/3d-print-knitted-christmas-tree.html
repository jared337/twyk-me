3D Print a Knitted Christmas Tree in Parts: Texture, Pins and Gold Rings

A Christmas tree that looks hand-knitted, made to print in parts. twyk's Texture tool knits the tree all over: little V stitches the same size all the way up, with fewer going round as it narrows, as a knitter decreases. The tree prints on one plate with holes for pins. The decorations print on a second plate: four gold rings that drop over the tree, each settling at its own height, and a star and 18 red and white baubles, each with a pin that pushes into its hole.

At a glance

Sizes
72 mm across, about 81 mm tall with the star.
Print settings
PLA, 0.16 mm layers so the stitches come out crisp, 2 walls, 10% infill. No supports on either plate: on plate 2 the rings and the star lie flat, and the baubles stand on their flats, pins up.

Build details

The knitted tree, step by step in the help: https://twyk.me/help#/t-knit-tree

Plate 1:
Base: cylinder, radius 36, height 4, at 0, 2, 0.
Tree: Taper, radius 30, top 2.6, height 64, lined up with Align → Centre and Centre, at height 36. Texture ▾ → Knit, size 5, depth 1.4, On: the sides.
Star hole: cylinder, radius 1.2, 8 deep, a Hole, centred, at height 64.5.
Bauble holes: cylinders, radius 1.2, 7.5 long, Holes, turned 90 to lie flat, in rings at heights 14, 32, 50 and 58 (6, 5, 4 and 3 round, made with Repeat ▾ → Ring). Then the tree and its holes merged (Ctrl+M).
Plate 2:
Rings: four Toruses, 1.5 thick, radius 30.6, 24.2, 16.4 and 6.2, laid flat one inside the other. On the tree they rest at heights 8, 23, 41 and 65.
Star: radius 9, inner 4, 3 thick, lying flat, with a pin: cylinder, radius 1, 9 long.
Baubles: sphere, radius 3, with a pin standing up (radius 1, 7.4 long) and a small flat cut under it.

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
