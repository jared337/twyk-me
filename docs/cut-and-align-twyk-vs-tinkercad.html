Two tools you reach for all the time when designing for 3D printing: cutting a part in two, and lining parts up. This video tries both in Tinkercad and in twyk, filmed for real, so you can see how each one gets the job done before you pick your tool.

Frequently asked questions

Does Tinkercad have a Cut tool?

Not in this video: Tinkercad has no Cut tool, so the usual way round it is used, with hole boxes. This is Tinkercad as it was when the video was filmed.

What does twyk add on top of Align?

As the video shows, twyk adds centring one part on the plate, Even margins, and Square to the grid.

Do I need to know Tinkercad to follow this?

No. The video shows each job in both tools, one after the other.
