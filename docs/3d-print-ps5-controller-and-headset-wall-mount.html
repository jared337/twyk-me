3D Print a PS5 Controller and Headset Wall Mount (Holds 2 DualSense)

A wall mount that holds two PS5 DualSense controllers and a headset: each controller sits on a tongue that slides in between its grips, and the headset hangs on a longer arm in the middle.

Project Desk & setup ⏱ 2:50
Start building → Watch on YouTube

At a glance

Sizes
plate 240 x 80 mm, controller tongues 30 mm wide and 55 mm long, 170 mm apart, a 110 mm headset arm, and two counterbored holes for 4 mm screws.
Print settings
PLA or PETG, 0.2 mm layers, 4 walls, 30% infill, printed lying on its back. Needs a 256 mm bed.
