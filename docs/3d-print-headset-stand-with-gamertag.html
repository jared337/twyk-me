3D Print a Headset Stand With Your Gamertag (SteelSeries Arctis Nova Pro)

A headset stand sized for the SteelSeries Arctis Nova Pro (it fits most over-ear headsets): a weighted base with your gamertag in raised letters, a 220 mm post, and a wide rounded saddle so the headband is not dented.

At a glance

Sizes
base 120 x 120 x 10 mm, post 28 mm across, saddle 100 mm wide. The gamertag is a separate object in its own colour, for a multi-colour printer.
Print settings
PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, base down, no supports.

Frequently asked questions

What sizes does this project use?

base 120 x 120 x 10 mm, post 28 mm across, saddle 100 mm wide. The gamertag is a separate object in its own colour, for a multi-colour printer.

What print settings should I use?

PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, base down, no supports.

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
