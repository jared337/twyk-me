3D Print a Phone Stand for Video Calls (Fits Your Case, Charger Gap)

A phone stand for video calls, built from scratch in a few minutes: it holds a phone with its case on (up to 12 mm thick), leans it back 25 degrees, and leaves a gap so the charger can stay plugged in, with a channel under the base for the cable.

At a glance

Sizes
base 90 x 90 mm, back rest 80 x 110 mm at 25 degrees, a 14 mm slot for the phone, a 16 mm gap for the charger.
Print settings
PLA, 0.2 mm layers, 3 walls, 15% infill, no supports, printed standing as modelled.

What sizes does this project use?

What print settings should I use?

Is twyk free to use?

Yes. twyk has no plans, payments or subscriptions. You sign in with a Google account and your models save to it as you go.

Can I change the design to fit what I own?

Yes. Every shape in twyk keeps settings you can change later, and you type sizes in millimetres or inches, so you can adjust this design to suit your own needs. The last chapter of the video, Make your own, shows how.
